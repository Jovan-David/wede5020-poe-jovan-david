Site addresses

  • Main garden and office

    45 Adelaide Tambo Drive, Durban North, Durban, 4051
    Open Tuesday to Saturday, 08:00 to 16:00.

  • Workshop and training hub

    Berea Community Hall, 121 Stephen Dlamini Road, Berea, Durban
    Workshops on Saturdays and selected weekdays.

  • Youth education plot

    Chatsworth Environmental Centre, 1 Havenside Drive, Chatsworth
    School visits Tuesday to Thursday, by booking.

  • Saturday harvest market

    Chartwell Drive, Umhlanga Rocks, Durban
    First Saturday of the month, 07:00 to 12:00.

Getting here: the M4 and M12 both pass within walking distance of the main garden, and there is a taxi rank 400 metres away on Adelaide Tambo Drive. Parking is available behind the tool shed, including two bays for visitors with a disability permit. The garden path is level and wheelchair friendly.

Growing fresh food and stronger neighbours in Durban

GreenThumb is a volunteer-run community garden in Durban North. We share land, tools and know-how so that every household can grow its own vegetables.

What you can do at GreenThumb

Whether you have ten minutes a week or a whole Saturday morning, there is a place for you in the garden.

Volunteering

Your first Saturday in the garden

Our weekly work party runs every Saturday from 08:00 to 11:00. You do not need any experience and you do not need to bring anything.

  • Gloves, tools and drinking water are provided.
  • Children are welcome with a parent or guardian.
  • Every volunteer takes home a bag of seasonal harvest.
  • Community service letters are issued on request.

No. More than half of our members had never grown vegetables before they joined. New members are paired with a mentor for their first season and can attend the beginner vegetable workshop at no cost.

A seasonal plot is R650 for six months and includes water, compost and shared tools. Four waist-height raised beds are available at R950 per season. Sponsorship places are available for households that cannot pay, and nobody is turned away for financial reasons.

The main garden at 45 Adelaide Tambo Drive is open from Tuesday to Saturday, 08:00 to 16:00. Guided tours run on the first Saturday of each month at 09:00 and must be booked through the enquiry form.

Yes. Our corporate team days accommodate ten people for R1 500 and include a facilitated programme, all materials and a lunch made from garden produce. Larger groups can be split across two visits.

Members keep what they grow. Any surplus is sold at the Saturday harvest market in Umhlanga, and one third of the income funds seedling packs for local primary schools.

Help us plant the next season

Every R100 buys seed and compost for one household for a month. Volunteers, sponsors and neighbours are all welcome.

Free guided garden tours

Tours take about 45 minutes and are led by one of our volunteer gardeners. You will see the seedling nursery, the composting area and the market beds.

  • When: first Saturday of every month at 09:00
  • Where: 45 Adelaide Tambo Drive, Durban North
  • Cost: free, but booking is essential
  • Bring: a hat, closed shoes and a water bottle

The garden path is level and wheelchair friendly, and there is parking behind the tool shed.

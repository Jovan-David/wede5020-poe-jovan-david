How booking works

Step 1

Choose a programme

Use the filters above to compare prices, dates and availability. Every card links straight to the enquiry form with that programme already selected.

Step 2

Send an enquiry

Tell us how many people are coming and which date suits you. You will see an estimated cost and the next available start before you send anything.

Step 3

We confirm within a day

A volunteer coordinator replies within one working day with a confirmed date, a payment reference and everything you need to bring.

Prices and dates: all amounts are in South African rand and include VAT where applicable. Workshop places are limited to 15 people so that everybody gets a turn with the tools.
